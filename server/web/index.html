Pair with Tesla

Enroll this controller as a Phone Key on the vehicle so it can send charging commands over BLE. A fresh key pair is generated on every pairing.

  1. Click Start pairing below.
  2. Walk to the car and sit inside (or stand nearby).
  3. Tap your NFC keycard on the center-console tray.
  4. Tap Confirm on the vehicle touchscreen.
